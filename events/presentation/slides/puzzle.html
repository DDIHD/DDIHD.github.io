Data-
centric

  • All user interactions with a model are through data
  • Mostly not technical term, but anything that goes in and comes out, and anything integrated in the training process
  • EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis) is the biggest and most time-consuming part of any custom AI application development
  • Data exploration and modelling assumptions are its most important aspects
  • It is an iterative process and key to understanding a model's aspects/behaviour
  • "Human"/domain decisions!

Technical
accessibility

  • Runs everywhere as a website: also as pre-installed app (PWA) that needs no admin rights on school computers and tablets
  • Data sovereignty is upheld by running only locally, not in the cloud
  • In the browser does not mean online: it can run off a USB stick or anywhere else
  • It is open source

Cognitive
accessibility

  • Low-key visual approach: no distinction between statistics and the data itself on essential entry level
  • Uses a recurring, known domain: the candy dataset
  • Scaffolding through adaptivity and progressive disclosure
  • Context switching is prevented by embedding it directly into the learning environment

Expansions

  • Visually low-key, but capable of most machine learning tasks on a reasonable scale
  • Can be extended for advanced high school courses or special seminars for talented students
  • Requests: exporting to programming environments, games, other use cases. Sandbox after curated learning path completed.
